Criminal Cases

This interactive tool, developed by the Justice Connection, showcases trends in case filing and clearance by the U.S. Department of Justice’s Legal Information Office Network System (LIONS). This dashboard highlights criminal cases filed by U.S. Attorney's Offices on behalf of the United States as plaintiff.

All data is from publicly released DOJ data, which ranges from October 1994 to July 2026 and will be updated on a monthly basis with information from LIONS. For more information about this data, see the footnote below or our data documentation page.
Program-category basis: Primary = each case counted once (first-entered code); All = counted in every category it touches.
