About this data: The DOJ National Caseload data dashboard is sourced and cleaned from monthly filings from the Department of Justice’s Legal Information Office Network System (LIONS).

LIONS data, also known as the DOJ Case Management System (CMS), is aggregated into a redacted annual fiscal year report at the close of the year. However, monthly data is provided in a 20 gigabyte file available to the public, with some categories redacted. This dashboard provides users a way to make data actionable and understood over time from 2012 to 2026.

LIONS data updates monthly, lagging by about two months. Monthly updates may include revised data from previous months, meaning updates may differ from previously posted numbers. For more information about the LIONS system, please visit the DOJ’s website.

Most information in the LIONS system is obtained during case intake and screening stages, according to the DOJ’s website. Any additional information is filed as the matter moves through proceedings.

For more details on program definitions and the legal statutes they include, please refer to the DOJ’s LIONS Code List here.
