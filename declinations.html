Criminal Matter Declinations

This interactive tool, developed by the Justice Connection, helps showcase trends in case filing and clearance by the U.S. Department of Justice' Legal Information Office Network System (LIONS). This dashboard highlights criminal matters declined by U.S. Attorney Offices on behalf of the United States.

All data is from publicly released DOJ data, which ranges from October 1994 to July 2026 and will be regularly updated on a monthly basis with information from LIONS. For more information about this data, see the footnote below or our data documentation page.
