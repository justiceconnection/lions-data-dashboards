Criminal Matter Declinations

This interactive tool, developed by the Justice Coalition, helps showcase trends in case filing and clearance by the U.S. Department of Justice' Legal Information Office Network System (LIONS). All data is from publicly released DOJ data, which ranges from October 1994 to June 2026 and will be regularly updated on a monthly basis with information from LIONS. For more information about this data, see the footnote below.

About this data: The DOJ National Caseload data dashboard is sourced and cleaned from monthly filings from the Department of Justice’s Legal Information Office Network System (LIONS). LIONS data, also known as the DOJ Case Management System (CMS), is aggregated into a redacted annual fiscal year report at the close of the year. However, monthly data is provided in a 20 gigabyte file available to the public, with some categories and identifying information redacted. This dashboard provides users a way to make data actionable and understood over time from 1994 to 2026. LIONS data updates monthly, lagging by about two months. Monthly updates may include revised data from previous months, meaning updates may differ from previously posted numbers. For more information about the LIONS system, please visit the DOJ’s website. Note not all DOJ cases are reflected in this data set because the system is used by U.S. attorneys offices (USAO), meaning it will exclude cases from Main Justice. Some inquiries are not logged into the system if they are considered “obviously trivial,” as well. Most information in the LIONS system is obtained during case intake and screening stages, according to the DOJ’s website. Any additional information is filed as the matter moves through proceedings.
