Matters declined — selected reasons over time

A declined matter is a criminal matter the U.S. Attorney chose not to prosecute (LIONS disposition "DE"), attributed to its first declination and that record's reason. Reasons follow the current EOUSA scheme (Annual Report Tables 14 & 15); the default view starts FY2015, when this seven-reason coding took effect (earlier matters used a legacy detailed coding folded into these categories, so pre-2015 figures are approximate). Break down by program category (Table 14; primary program group) or referring agency (Table 15; lead investigative agency, Table 3D groups). Top chart stacks the selected reasons; bottom chart shows them as separate lines; both are filtered to the selected districts and categories/agencies. Shaded bands = administrations.
