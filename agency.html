Cases by Referring Agency

This interactive tool, developed by the Justice Connection, showcases trends in case filing and clearance by the U.S. Department of Justice's Legal Information Office Network System (LIONS). This dashboard shows cases by the referring government agencies.

All data is from publicly released DOJ data, which ranges from October 1994 to July 2026 and will be regularly updated on a monthly basis with information from LIONS. For more information about this data, see the footnote below or our data documentation page.
Agency basis: Lead = each case counted once under its first-listed referring agency; All = counted under every agency it names (~2% of cases name more than one).
