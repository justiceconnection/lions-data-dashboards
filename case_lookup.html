About this data: The DOJ National Caseload data dashboard is sourced and cleaned from monthly filings from the Department of Justice’s Legal Information Office Network System (LIONS). Justice Connection does not manipulate the data or conduct any analysis of the data; this dashboard is a data management tool that allows users to investigate the data as its presented in the DOJ’s distribution. Data validation is possible by comparing the DOJ’s annual filings to monthly data downloads. However, given the manual input processes of the LIONS system and lack of information due to redaction or sealed cases, it’s important to note that all data cannot be independently validated. LIONS data updates monthly. Monthly updates include revised data from previous months, meaning updates may differ from previously posted numbers. Based on data analysis, Justice Connection estimates that a near-complete picture of monthly filings can be assumed after a three-month lag. In other words, for the most accurate monthly filing data, users should only analyze data from three months prior, because more cases data will be added in the subsequent two updates.
