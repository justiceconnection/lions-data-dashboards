About this data: The DOJ National Caseload data dashboard is sourced and cleaned from monthly filings from the Department of Justice’s Legal Information Office Network System (LIONS). LIONS data, also known as the DOJ Case Management System (CMS), is aggregated into a redacted annual fiscal year report at the close of the year. However, monthly data is provided in a 88-gigabyte file available to the public, with some categories and identifying information redacted. This dashboard provides users a way to make data actionable and understood over time from 1994 to 2026. Justice Connection does not manipulate the data or conduct any analysis of the data; this dashboard is a data management tool that allows users to investigate the data as its presented in the DOJ’s distribution. Data validation is possible by comparing the DOJ’s annual filings to monthly data downloads. However, given the manual input processes of the LIONS system and lack of information due to redaction or sealed cases, it’s important to note that all data cannot be independently validated. LIONS data updates monthly. Monthly updates include revised data from previous months, meaning updates may differ from previously posted numbers. Based on data analysis, Justice Connection estimates that a near-complete picture of monthly filings can be assumed after a three-month lag. In other words, for the most accurate monthly filing data, users should only analyze data from three months prior, because more cases data will be added in the subsequent two updates. Most information in the LIONS system is obtained during case intake and screening stages, according to the DOJ’s website. Any additional information is filed as the matter moves through proceedings. For more information about the LIONS system, please visit the DOJ’s website. Note not all DOJ cases are reflected in this data set because the system is used by U.S. attorneys offices (USAO), meaning it will exclude cases from Main Justice. Some inquiries are not logged into the system if they are considered “obviously trivial,” as well. The United States’ role is determined by the filing attorney. The LIONS system defaults to plaintiff but in addition to plaintiff or defendant could range from creditor, counsel cost plus contractor, counsel for government employee, third party defendant, counsel for Native American, third party plaintiff, intervenor, counsel for veteran or other. Matters are “a referral on which an attorney spends one hour or more and for which formal papers have not been filed with the Court,” whereas a case reflects action taken in court, such as a formal complaint or petition, according to the DOJ User Manual.
